# Privacy Policy for Nook

**Last updated: 21 August 2026**

Nook is made by TTB INTERNATIONAL LLC. This policy explains what the app does
with your information. The short version: Nook has no accounts and no servers
of ours, and we don't collect anything about you.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Nook does not collect, transmit, or store any personal
information on our servers, because we don't operate any. There is no sign-up,
no account, and no analytics or tracking of any kind in the app.

## What stays on your device

Everything you put into Nook, your focus session history, the task labels you
type, your timer durations, and your alarm sound choice, is stored on your
iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. It is never uploaded anywhere.
Deleting the app removes it.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Nook schedules them locally on your device,
through iOS itself, to tell you when a focus session or break ends. These
never pass through a server we control, because none exists.

## If sync ever arrives

A future version of Nook may offer optional cross-device sync. If it does, it
will use Apple's CloudKit under your own iCloud account: the same private
system Apple uses for iCloud backups, and it will be opt-in. This policy will
be updated first, and nothing will change for you unless you turn it on.

## Children

Nook is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including
children.

## Third parties

Nook contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers.
It makes no network requests at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
with a new date at the top.
